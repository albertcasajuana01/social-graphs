    Week 6 · From counts to meaning New

    Names or story? Splitting every Marvel lookalike into what it shares

    A cosine similarity is a sum over words, so we can say exactly how much of every match comes from shared names. For 98% of pages the TF-IDF lookalike is mostly a name: Storm matches the Human Torch on one word. Take the names away and three quarters of the lookalikes change. The new ones are symbiotes, vampires and powered armor, often between pages Wikipedia never linked.

    Week 5 · From language to numbers

    Wikipedia copies the paperwork, not the plot

    The Marvel pages get their text. We hunt for passages that appear word for word on two pages: they are publication histories, creator anecdotes and reading lists, almost never the fictional biographies. Every copied paragraph sits between pages that are already linked.

    Where did the Renaissance go? 200 Louvain runs and the philosophers no tradition will keep

    We switch to the philosopher network and run Louvain 200 times. Almost everyone keeps their tradition, except Galileo, Kepler, Bruno and the other Renaissance naturalists: a tight bloc that a different tradition absorbs each run.
